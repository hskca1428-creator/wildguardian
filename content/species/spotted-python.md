# Spotted Python (*Antaresia maculosa*)

The Spotted Python, *Antaresia maculosa*, is a non-venomous Australian python. [1] [2] WildGuardian’s LOW label means the database does not classify it as a venomous medical risk; it does not mean an animal should be handled. Every wild snake can bite when frightened or restrained, and a visual match should never be used to decide that an unknown snake is safe to touch.

The safest response to a snake sighting is to stop, keep a comfortable distance and give it a clear route away. Do not corner, catch, relocate or kill a snake. Queensland guidance specifically warns that snake identification can be difficult and advises people not to assume a snake is non-venomous. [2]

## Identification clues and limits

Spotted Pythons usually have a yellowish or light-brown base colour with irregular dark-brown blotches running from the head to the tail. The blotches may form sinuous markings behind the head and near the tail base. [1] Queensland regional guidance similarly describes a brown snake with dark brown blotches. [2]

That pattern is a useful starting point only. Markings vary, and Australia has other patterned pythons and snakes. This profile does not provide a close-range identification method. Do not lift an animal, move it from a crevice or take an unnecessary close photograph to confirm head plates or heat-sensing pits.

The scientific name matters because *Antaresia childreni* is a different species. The Atlas of Living Australia treats *A. maculosa* as the Spotted Python, and the Australian Museum notes that some older Lizard Island records assigned to *A. childreni* were misidentifications. [1] [3]

## Habitat and ecology

Queensland sources place the species in eucalypt woodland through to rainforest, and identify it as mainly nocturnal. [2] [4] The Australian Museum records a preference for rocky hillsides and outcrops with crevices and caves, with an occasional observation of an animal using building rafters on Lizard Island. [1]

It feeds on reptiles, birds and small mammals. [2] The Australian Museum records bats taken near cave entrances, which is consistent with a python using rocky shelter and crevices in its hunting environment. [1] These details explain why a Spotted Python may be seen after dark around rocks, buildings or habitat edges, but should not be used to predict whether one is present.

Queensland Government regional profiles document the species in south-east Queensland, the Cairns region and the Townsville region. [2] [4] [5] The broad database range is a guide rather than a substitute for a local fauna record.

## Safer encounters and bites

Watch from a distance and leave the snake space to move away. Keep children and pets back. If the animal is in a building or another place where this is not possible, seek help from an appropriately licensed local wildlife or snake-removal service.

Even though this python is non-venomous, do not dismiss a bite or rely on a species guess. Healthdirect advises that all snake bites are potentially life-threatening because the snake may be misidentified. [6] Call 000, keep the person still and calm, apply pressure immobilisation to the affected limb and await an ambulance. Do not wash, cut or suck the wound, apply a tourniquet, or try to catch the snake. [6]

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a Spotted Python venomous?

Queensland Government classifies the species as non-venomous. [2] It can still bite defensively, and any unidentified snake should be given space.

### Where does it live?

It uses woodland, rainforest and rocky areas with crevices or caves, and can occasionally use built structures such as rafters. [1] [2]

### Can I pick one up because it is a python?

No. Handling can stress or injure the animal and provoke a defensive bite. A python identification is not a handling recommendation.

### What if a person is bitten by a snake thought to be a Spotted Python?

Call 000 and follow snake-bite first aid. Healthdirect advises treating all snake bites as potentially life-threatening because a visual identification can be wrong. [6]
